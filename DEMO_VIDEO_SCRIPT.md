# 🎬 ChainPaws: 2-Minute Demo Video Walkthrough Script

> **Target Duration:** 2 minutes to 2 minutes 15 seconds  
> **Live App URL:** [https://chain-paws.vercel.app](https://chain-paws.vercel.app)  
> **Recommended Screen Setup:** Fullscreen browser (1920x1080) with Devnet cluster ready.

---

## ⏱️ Scene-by-Scene Director's Cut

### **[0:00 – 0:25] Scene 1: The Hook & The Problem**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * Start on the **ChainPaws Home Screen / Live Map**.
  * Hover over the hero headline: *"Protecting Pets with Trustless Bounties"*.
  * Slowly zoom into the glowing map pins across the US.

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"One in every three family pets will go missing in their lifetime. But when heartbroken pet parents put up posters offering cash rewards, over 70% of responses are extortion scams demanding money upfront before sending proof of life.*
  > 
  > *Meanwhile, legacy microchip databases are siloed private databases that don't talk to each other.*
  > 
  > *This weekend, for International Dog Day, I built **ChainPaws** — a decentralized, zero-database pet identification registry and trustless escrow recovery network running natively on Solana."*

---

### **[0:25 – 0:50] Scene 2: Live Geospatial Discovery Radar**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * Scroll down to the **Live Missing Pet Discovery Radar**.
  * Click on city buttons: **San Francisco** $\rightarrow$ **Los Angeles** $\rightarrow$ **Austin** (showing the smooth map camera pan).
  * Click on a glowing red marker pin (e.g. *Coco* or *Atlas*) to open the pet summary card.

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"ChainPaws has no centralized backend database — Solana is the sole source of truth. Every companion's microchip is hashed via SHA-256 into an immutable Program Derived Account.*
  > 
  > *On the Live Recovery Radar, real-time OpenStreetMap telemetry tracks missing alerts across major metro hubs with pulsing medical-urgency rings, allowing anyone in the community to immediately report a sighting or submit a rescue claim."*

---

### **[0:50 – 1:15] Scene 3: AI Multilingual Flyer & Voice Siren Broadcast**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * On the pet card (e.g. *Coco*), click the **Flyer (📄)** icon to open the **Emergency Bulletin Modal**.
  * Click the language pills: **EN** $\rightarrow$ **ES** $\rightarrow$ **JA** (show the text dynamically translating into Japanese with the *✨ AI Multilingual Engine* badge).
  * Click **Play Voice Siren (JA)** for 3 seconds to let the audio alert speak aloud.

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"When a companion goes missing, every second counts. Tapping the emergency bulletin summons Google's Gemini AI to dynamically translate the alert into 5 languages on demand.*
  > 
  > *Look at that: it translates critical medical tags — like daily heart medication — naturally into Japanese and Spanish. And with the browser voice siren, neighborhood search parties can broadcast an audible alert directly on the street."*

---

### **[1:15 – 1:40] Scene 4: Solana Non-Custodial Escrow & Settlement**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * Click the **My Pets** tab in the top navigation bar.
  * Show the registered dog with status: **`MISSING (Escrow Locked: 1.50 SOL)`**.
  * Click **Confirm & Pay SOL** (or trigger the recovery settlement flow with victory confetti 🎉).

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Now for the core innovation: trustless escrow. When an owner reports a dog missing, they lock a SOL reward into an autonomous on-chain escrow vault PDA.*
  > 
  > *No company or middleman touches your funds. When a Good Samaritan or partner clinic scans the microchip, the smart contract programmatically transfers the bounty directly to the finder's wallet, reclaims state rent, and resets the status to Safe. Zero scams. Zero intermediary fees."*

---

### **[1:40 – 2:00] Scene 5: Guardian Badges & Collective Rescue Impact**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * Click the **Badges** tab in the top navigation bar to showcase the 5 glowing achievement badges and live network impact statistics.

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Every rescue permanently unlocks on-chain Guardian Badges and updates our collective network impact metrics — uniting pet parents, finders, and veterinary clinics into a verifiable, scam-free recovery network."*

### **[2:00 – 2:15] Scene 6: Sign-Off**

* **🖥️ Screen Action:**
  * Return to the sleek **Live Map view**.
  * Highlight the top bar with the Solana Devnet badge and GitHub repo link.

* **🎙️ Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Built with Rust Anchor, Next.js, and Google AI on Solana. Because our best friends deserve more than paper flyers and extortion scams — they deserve a cryptographic guarantee.*
  > 
  > *This is ChainPaws. Happy International Dog Day."*

## 💡 Quick Tips for High-Impact Delivery
1. **Pacing:** Keep your tone warm, confident, and energetic.
2. **Audio Sync:** At `[1:10]`, let the browser voice siren speak for 2–3 seconds so the judges hear the audio broadcast clearly.
3. **Publishing:** Upload the recording as **Unlisted or Public** on YouTube or Loom, and copy the link into line 51 of `DEV_SUBMISSION_ARTICLE.md`.
